Step-by-step instructions

Tap an action to open it. Every record gets an owner, and every task gets a due date.

ACreate the companyStep 3 · Pavithra / Chrispin · as soon as a call shows interest›
  1. Search HubSpot for the brand name and website first, so you don't create a duplicate.
  2. Go to CRM › Companies and click Create company.
  3. Fill in the fields below and click Create.

CAdd a noteSteps 3 to 9 · after every conversation›
  1. Open the company record (brand notes live on the company, so everyone sees the full history).
  2. Click Notes › Create note.
  3. Write the note using the format below.
  4. Under Associated with, tick the contact and the deal (if there is one) so the note shows on all three records.
  5. Click Create note.
Date / channel: [e.g. 29 Oct, Skyfall call] Spoke with: [name, role] What we learnt: [platform, order volume, current loyalty tool, pain points] What they want: [their ask in one line] Agreed next step: [what, who, by when]
Skyfall calls: Skyfall is not linked to HubSpot, so nothing arrives automatically. The disposition and recording stay in Skyfall. In HubSpot, use Calls › Log call on the contact, pick the outcome and paste the summary. Include the call date and time so anyone can find the recording in Skyfall.
DCreate the dealStep 3 · Pavithra / Chrispin · on every call that shows interest›
  1. Open the company record.
  2. In the right sidebar, find Deals and click + Add › Create new. This links the deal to the company.
  3. Fill in the fields below.
  4. Under Associate deal with, add the contact(s) you spoke to.
  5. Click Create. The deal now shows in the MQL column of the deals board.
  6. If discovery was booked on the same call, go straight on to E and move the deal to SQL.

EDiscovery booked: move to SQL, send email and inviteStep 4 · Pavithra / Chrispin · copy Vaishnavi and Felix›
  1. Agree the date and time with the brand on the call.
  2. In HubSpot, move the deal from MQL to SQL (see H). Set the contact's Lifecycle stage to Sales Qualified Lead and Lead status to Open Deal.
  3. Send the calendar invite with the brand contact(s), Vaishnavi and Felix as guests. Title: RewardRally discovery | Brand name.
  4. Send the confirmation email below to the brand, with Vaishnavi and Felix in CC.
  5. Make sure both show on the HubSpot contact. If they do not appear automatically, log them with Email › Log email and Meetings › Log meeting.
  6. Create a task for Vaishnavi on the deal: Discovery: Brand name, due on the meeting date (see G).
Subject: RewardRally discovery call | [Brand name], [Day, Date] at [Time] Hi [First name], Thanks for your time on the call today. As agreed, here are the details for our discovery session: Date and time: [Day, Date], [Time] IST Link: [meeting link] (calendar invite sent) Duration: [duration] Vaishnavi from our team will take you through how RewardRally works for Shopify brands like yours, and we would like to understand your customers, order volumes and what you want from a loyalty program. I've copied Vaishnavi and Felix here. If the time no longer works, just reply and we will move it. Regards, [Your name] RewardRally
FLog the discovery outcomeStep 5 · Vaishnavi · same day as the meeting›
  1. Open the deal. Click Meetings and set the meeting outcome to Completed (or No show / Rescheduled).
  2. Add a note on the company (see C) with the discovery format below.
  3. Move the deal from SQL to Follow Up, and update amount and close date (see H). Set the contact's Lifecycle stage to Opportunity.
  4. Create the follow-up tasks for Pavithra or Chrispin (see G), one task per next step.
Discovery: [Brand], [Date] Attendees: [names, roles] Store: [Shopify plan, monthly orders, AOV] Current loyalty setup: [none / tool name / issues] Goals: [repeat purchase, referrals, reviews and so on] Decision maker and process: [who signs, by when] Fit and recommended plan: [plan, price] Next steps: [what, owner, date]
GCreate a task or reminderAny step · everyone · every next action is a task›
  1. Open the deal (or the company if there is no deal yet) and click Tasks › Create task.
  2. Fill in the fields below and click Create.
  3. When the task is done, tick it complete and add a note (see C). Then create the next task. A deal should never be left without an open task.

HMove the deal stageSteps 5 to 7 · whoever owns the stage›
  1. Open the deal, or go to CRM › Deals in board view.
  2. Change Deal stage (or drag the card) using the stage table above.
  3. Update Amount and Close date whenever they change.
  4. For Closed Lost, Cold or On-Hold, add a note saying why.
ISchedule the onboarding meetingStep 7 · Pavithra / Chrispin›
  1. Agree a time with the brand, Vaishnavi and the product team.
  2. Send the calendar invite: RewardRally onboarding | Brand name, with the brand contacts, Vaishnavi and the product team only.
  3. In HubSpot, move the deal to Live and set Lifecycle stage to Customer on both the contact and the company.
  4. Log the meeting on the deal and add a note with plan, price and start date.
  5. Create the first billing task (see K) and the first review task for Vaishnavi (see J).
JSet up performance reviewsStep 8 · Vaishnavi · bi-weekly or monthly›
  1. Ask the customer whether they want reviews every two weeks or every month.
  2. On the company, create a task Performance review: Brand assigned to Vaishnavi, and set it to Repeat every 2 weeks or every month.
  3. After each review, add a note: key numbers shared, customer feedback, actions agreed.
KBilling follow-upsStep 9 · Pavithra / Chrispin›
  1. On the company, create a repeating task Billing: Brand on the billing date, assigned to the JE.
  2. When the invoice is sent, add a note with the invoice number and amount.
  3. If unpaid after the due date, create a Call task for the next day and note every reminder sent.
LDaily HubSpot reviewEveryone · start of every day›
  1. Go to CRM › Tasks and open the Due today and Overdue views, filtered to Assigned to: me.
  2. Work through them top to bottom. Click Start tasks to go through them one after another.
  3. After each one: mark it complete, add a note, and create the next task.
  4. Before logging off, check that every open deal you own has a future task.

House rules

  • No pickup or no interest: no HubSpot record. The disposition stays in Skyfall.
  • Every interested call gets a company, contact and MQL deal, entered by hand.
  • Search before you create. No duplicate companies or contacts.
  • No open deal without a future task.
  • Notes go on the company, associated to the contact and deal.
  • Vaishnavi and Felix are on every discovery invite and every follow-up.
