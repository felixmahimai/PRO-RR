# RewardRally Outbound Workflow

Interactive guide to the RewardRally outbound sales workflow at Peninsular Research Operations.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It covers:

- Who does what (Pavithra and Chrispin, Vaishnavi, Felix)
- An interactive flowchart with a step-by-step walkthrough
- Stage-by-stage owners and HubSpot updates
- Which HubSpot deal column to use (MQL, SQL, Follow Up and onward)
- Step-by-step HubSpot instructions with copyable email and note templates
- Email campaign track (Apollo list, Bouncer, Brevo) and house rules

Tools: Apollo (leads), Skyfall (calling, not linked to HubSpot), HubSpot (CRM), Bouncer and Brevo (email campaigns).
